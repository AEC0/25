Hey Amulya,

It's honestly a little crazy to think that we've known each other since 8th standard. From school days all the way through PUC, and then four years of engineering in completely different places — you've been around for a surprisingly large part of my life.

A lot has changed. We went to different colleges, got busy, had different routines, and there were definitely phases when we didn't talk regularly. But somehow, the friendship stayed there in the background. And whenever we meet again, it never really feels like we're starting from zero.

Life doesn't always go according to plan, but I hope this next chapter is a little kinder, a little lighter, and full of people and moments that make you happy.
